Privacy notice — Observatory Dashboard

This notice describes how the Observatory Dashboard processes personal data when you sign in and use it. We process your personal data in accordance with the applicable data protection law, in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Brandenburg Data Protection Act (BbgDSG).

The live camera images shown on the dashboard are covered by a separate privacy notice on the camera surveillance (German). This notice covers the dashboard itself: signing in, the session, and the observing session log.

Purpose of the processing

The Observatory Dashboard shows the current operating state of the University Observatory and coordinates its use. It records which person is currently observing, so that several users do not operate the telescope at the same time, and it keeps a log of completed observing sessions so that the use of the instrument can be traced afterwards.

Access is restricted to a defined group of people entrusted with the operation of the observatory. The sign-in exists to enforce that restriction and to protect the technical installations against unauthorised access. Processing is limited to what this requires. The dashboard performs no reach measurement, no analysis of user behaviour and no profiling.

Legal basis

The legal basis for the processing is Art. 6(1)(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with § 5(1) BbgDSG in conjunction with § 3(1) BbgHG in conjunction with § 26(1) BbgDSG.

The processing is necessary for the performance of the tasks of the University of Potsdam and for the protection of the technical installations it operates.

What data is processed

Sign-in against the directory service

To sign in, you enter the user name and password of your observatory account. The dashboard does not store your password; it passes the credentials to the observatory directory service (LDAP) over an encrypted connection in order to verify them.

During this check the dashboard reads the following attributes of your directory entry: user identifier (uid), name (cn, givenName, sn), e-mail address (mail) and your group memberships. Of these, only your user identifier, your display name and the group memberships relevant to access are kept, for the duration of your session.

Session cookie

After a successful sign-in the dashboard sets one cookie named ost_status_session. It is technically necessary: without it you would have to sign in again for every page. The cookie is cryptographically signed and contains your user identifier, your display name and the group memberships relevant to access. It is marked HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, is valid for a maximum of 12 hours, and is deleted when you sign out.

No other cookies are set. The public camera privacy notice and the status endpoint used for uptime monitoring can be opened without any cookie being set.

Observing session log

The entries in the observing session log do not come from the sign-in described above. Observing sessions are started at the Observatory Management System, the control computer in the observatory: when a session begins, a dialog there asks for a name and, optionally, the object to be observed and the planned duration. What is entered in that dialog is what the dashboard displays while the session runs and what is written to the log when it ends.

Recorded are the designation entered there, the observed target, the start and end time, the duration of the session, and the reason it ended. Signed-in users can view this log on the dashboard. The purpose is to make the use of the instrument traceable and to allow technical faults during unattended operation to be reconstructed afterwards.

Entering a name there is voluntary, and a full name is neither required nor wanted. The entry is free text and is not checked against your account. A first name is sufficient and is the customary entry; for guided observations a description of the group is sufficient. Please enter no more than is needed to tell concurrent users apart.

Log files

Each access to the dashboard is recorded in the web server log files for the purpose of operational and data security: IP address, time of access, the page or content element requested, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, user agent (browser and operating system used) and, where supplied, the referrer URL.

In addition, the application records each sign-in, failed sign-in attempt, lockout and sign-out in the system log, together with the user name entered and the IP address of the client.

Protection against automated sign-in attempts

To protect accounts against systematic password guessing, failed sign-in attempts are counted per user name and IP address. After five failed attempts, further attempts from that combination are refused for a limited period. This counter is held in memory only and is not written to disk. Independently of this, repeated failed attempts can cause the IP address to be blocked temporarily at the web server (two hours).

Storage period

The web server log files and the sign-in entries in the system log are stored for a maximum of seven days and are deleted afterwards.

The session cookie expires after a maximum of 12 hours, or earlier when you sign out.

The counter of failed sign-in attempts is discarded after a few minutes at the latest, and in any case when the service is restarted.

Entries in the observing session log are retained for as long as the documentation of the observatory's use requires. They are not deleted automatically.

Obligation to provide data

You are not obliged to provide personal data. However, the dashboard can only be used with an observatory account; without a sign-in the observing state and the camera images are not accessible.

The name entered when an observing session is started at the Observatory Management System is voluntary in particular. It is free text, it is not checked against your account, and it need not identify you personally — see Observing session log above.

Recipients of the data

The personal data is not transmitted to external bodies.

Access to the dashboard is limited to a small group of people entrusted with the operation and technical support of the observatory. Access to the log files is limited to the administrators of the system. Access is protected against unauthorised access by appropriate technical and organisational measures.

Transfer to third countries

Your personal data is not transferred to countries outside the European Union or the European Economic Area.

Automated decision-making

Automated decision-making, including profiling, does not take place. The temporary lockout after repeated failed sign-in attempts is a purely technical protective measure and produces no legal effects concerning you.

Your rights

You have the right to request information from us about the processing of personal data concerning you. In addition to a copy of the personal data, this right of access covers information on the purpose of the processing, the recipients of the data and the storage period.

If incorrect personal data is processed, you may request that we rectify it. Where the statutory conditions of Art. 17 or Art. 18 GDPR are met, you are also entitled in principle to erasure of personal data or to restriction of processing. Please note that restricted processing of the data may not be possible in all cases. Under the conditions of Art. 20 GDPR you have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, or to request that it be transmitted to another controller.

Insofar as the processing is permitted without your consent, you may object to it under the conditions of Art. 21 GDPR.
